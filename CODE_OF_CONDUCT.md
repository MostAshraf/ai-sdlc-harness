# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Everyone who participates in this project — filing issues, reviewing, contributing code, or discussing it — is entitled to a harassment-free experience, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct about the work and considerate about the person. Criticize the design, the diff, or the decision — never the contributor.
- Assume good faith. Most disagreements here are about missing context, not bad intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully. "This breaks on Windows because X" is a gift; treat it as one.
- Own your mistakes, fix them, and move on. Nobody keeps score.
- Respect that maintainer time is finite. A closed issue or a declined PR is a scoping decision, not a judgment of you.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language or imagery, or sexual attention of any kind.
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing someone's private information — physical or email address, or anything else — without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions, and commit history — and whenever someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer via [the contact on the GitHub profile](https://github.com/MostAshraf). Reports are handled confidentially; the reporter's identity is not disclosed.

The maintainer will review and respond as promptly as circumstances allow, and may take any action deemed appropriate — from a private warning, to editing or removing comments, issues, and PRs, to a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces. The reasoning behind a moderation decision may be explained, but the decision itself is not up for public debate.
